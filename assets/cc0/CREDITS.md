# Credits for third-party assets in assets/cc0/

Everything in this folder comes from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com) and is licensed CC0 1.0 (public domain: free to use, change and ship, no attribution required). The authors are listed anyway.
Downloaded with tools/cc0_fetch.tscn; each asset folder also keeps the site's own record in asset_info.json.
Rule for this project: only CC0 or original material goes in here. Nothing from other games.

## Characters and animations (assets/cc0/packs/, all CC0 1.0 by Quaternius, https://quaternius.com)

- Universal Base Characters [Standard] (`universal_base_characters_standard`): rigged bodies, heads, eyes, hairstyles. Adult bodies only are used.
- Modular Character Outfits - Fantasy [Standard] (`modular_character_outfits_fantasy_standard`): Peasant and Ranger outfits.
- Universal Animation Library [Standard] (`universal_animation_library`) and Universal Animation Library 2 [Standard] (`universal_animation_library_2`): clips baked onto our skeletons by tools/anim_bake.tscn into assets/anim/.
- Each pack's own licence file is kept in its folder. Zips are downloaded by hand into assets/cc0/_zips/ and unpacked by tools/pack_fetch.tscn.

Sea 9a fix: leaving a dungeon puts you back where you went in

Coming out of a dungeon returns the player to the spot he stood on when he entered (facing away from the gate) instead of a ray-found gate front, and holds him there until the island's solid ground is built under him, so he never drops through or lands low. Leaving the sea while inside a dungeon (painting, /tp, the session) restores the sea's look and hides the dungeon lamp.

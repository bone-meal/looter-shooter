Round 6 step 1: Counter-Strike style movement everywhere

Ground friction and acceleration, air strafing, a 57 unit jump, jump
fatigue and a bunny-hop cap as pure rules (core/move_rules.gd) with every
number in data/movement.json in Source units. The player moves in fixed
64 Hz ticks and the view glides between them. Each gun body has its own
top speed (sniper slower while aiming), sprint is kept, crouch is on C.
New boots item phoons_treasure: hops while jump is held, no fatigue.
Speed readout with the debug HUD; tools/move_check.tscn checks the rules.

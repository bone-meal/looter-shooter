Round 6 step 7: island power-ups, pony rule, spectating while out

Power-ups (islands): a dying enemy leaves one with 3 percent chance, at most
4 a round; an island boss always does; the last enemy of a special round
leaves a max ammo. Four kinds in data/powerups.json: max ammo, insta-kill
(30 s, never a boss), double points (30 s), nuke (everything ashore dies,
a boss loses a quarter, 400 points each). Whoever walks into one takes it
for the whole party. Pure rules core/powerup_rules.gd, pickups pooled in
game/powerup_field.gd, HUD call-out and timers, three new sounds, admin
command /power, check tools/powerup_check.tscn. Online the host decides
drops, pickups and what one blow kills (game/net_rounds.gd).

Pony rule: the pony tier is no longer rolled by enemies, chests or casino
crates (weight 0 in data/gun_loot.json); any non-boss enemy anywhere leaves
a pony gun 1 kill in 100000 (data/drops.json pony_trash_one_in).

Director's changes to step 6: a revived player has full health; a player
who is out watches the others (camera follows a teammate, shoot = next) and
appears at the spawn when the next round starts; the save menu shows each
character's best round.

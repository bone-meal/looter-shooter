Round 6 step 13: the main quest, ten hidden songs and the jukebox

The Drowned Bell, a seven-step quest across the islands: read the log book,
bring the power back, fill the brine jars with kills by the wreck, shoot out
three old lamps, build the boat, ring the bell on Gull Rock and beat what
answers it. Every word lives in data/zombies/writing/quest.txt so the story
can be rewritten without touching code; the steps and places are in
data/zombies/quest.json. The host decides progress, it resets at game over,
and each completion gives a random hand-made unique plus points.

Ten hidden songs: three glass floats each are hidden on the islands; shooting
all three unlocks and plays that song. The songs are the director's own files
dropped into assets/music as Artist - Title.mp3 (ogg and wav work too). The
club has a jukebox that plays any song unlocked by the character.

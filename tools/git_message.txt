Round 6 step 11: colosseum tickets, loot by wave, armour drops, 22 modifiers

Tickets (data/colosseum.json, core/colosseum_rules.gd, game/colosseum_board.gd):
the painting opens a board; 4 / 8 / 12 waves for 2000 / 5000 / 10000 points;
the buyer pays and the party goes in. Every wave is tougher (+12 % health,
+8 % damage, +1 % speed a wave). A cleared run ends with the chest and the
door; the arena no longer restarts by itself.

Loot by wave: ordinary kills leave a gun 3 % of the time (green in waves 1-4,
blue 5-7, purple 8-9, gold 10-12, a small chance of one tier better), elites
always, bosses one tier above their wave, the wave-12 boss three guns (orange
60 / rainbow 30 / pony 10) and sometimes Phoon's Treasure. The chest holds two
guns one tier above the run's last wave. No greys.

Armour now drops in the colosseum as things lying in the world
(game/loot_field.gd spawn_item); rarely as the variant with a holster: one
more ready gun slot (core/item_db.gd variants, data/items.json).

22 modifiers ticked on the board: each makes the run harder and every loot
chance better; core/colosseum_rules.gd effects() turns them into one table
read by the wave director, enemies, the player, the HUD and the session.

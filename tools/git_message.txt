Round 6 step 15: the hop course, HUD boxes and a boat fix

A practice course for the movement floats high over the sea, reached
through a stone arch on Home Sand (or /tp course): 20 platforms in four
stages (Warm-up, The Bend, Stepping Stones and The Leap, which needs air
strafing). The clock starts when you leave the start pad, checkpoints keep
your place after a fall, and the best time is saved with the character.
On the course everybody moves the same: no speed bonus, perks or auto-hop
boots.

A faint grey box now sits behind the chat lines in the bottom left and
behind the points (yours and the party's) so the text stays readable.

Boat: sailed to the edge of the islands' waters (toward the colosseum or
the club) it got stuck for good, with the crew in it. It now checks the
end that goes first, so it stops at the edge and can always back off; and
the club is no longer built while sailing near that edge.

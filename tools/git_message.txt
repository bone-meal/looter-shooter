Round 6 step 6: going down, the backpack left behind, the bank

Alone, running out of health on the islands ends the run: a game-over
screen shows the round reached and the character's best round, and
everything carried stays in a backpack where you lay (worn equipment and
the knife stay on you, a pistol goes into your hand). Take it back with
the interact key; go down again first and the old backpack is lost.
A letter box on the spawn island is the bank: 10 slots that are safe when
you go down, 20 and 30 for points, up to 60 for casino coins.
Online a player goes down for 30 seconds and a teammate standing by can
help them up; nobody came = out until the next round; nobody left
standing = game over for the party (game/net_downs.gd; written, not yet
run between two machines). Deaths in the colosseum still cost nothing.

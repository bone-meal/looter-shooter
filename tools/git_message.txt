Round 6 step 3: points, round HUD in chalk, Tab scoreboard

Zombies-style points saved with the character: a hit pays 10, the killing
blow 60, a headshot kill 100, a melee kill 130 (data/points.json). Enemies
have a head now: the top sixth of their box, hit 1.5 times as hard.
The HUD shows the round in red chalk (tallies to 5, then a number; the
colosseum as 3 / 12), enemies left, the points with pop-ups and the
party's points. Holding Tab shows the scoreboard; online each machine
reports its own row and the host cleans and passes the table on.
No points in Aurelind. tools/score_check.tscn checks the rules.

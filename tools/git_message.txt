Round 6 step 14: crosshairs, inspect key and photo mode

Settings now has a Crosshair page: 30 ready-made crosshairs shown as
pictures, sliders for every setting (length, thickness, gap, centre dot,
outline, ring, turn, arms, colour, opacity) and a share code to copy to a
friend and paste back. The choice is saved with the settings and the HUD
draws it.

The inspect key (V) looks over what is in hand: a move of its own for each
of the 8 gun bodies, each of the 13 knife types and the staff in Aurelind.
Shooting, aiming, reloading or switching stops it.

Photo mode (F10): the screens go away and a free camera flies near the
character (shown in third person); alone the game stands still. The shoot
key saves a picture into the Pictures folder, Looter Shooter Photos.

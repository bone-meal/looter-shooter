Round 6 step 9a: the power switch and fifteen perk machines

Power: three parts lie on Home Sand; with all three the switch is built and
thrown at the workbench on Painter's Key. Perk machines work only while the
power is on; game over turns it off and puts the parts back.

Perks (data/perks.json, limit six, all lost on going down): Second Wind,
Hair Trigger, Trade Winds, Blast Proof on Home Sand; Iron Gut, Steady Hand,
Deep Pockets, Run and Gun on Painter's Key; Quickhands, Scavenger,
Backdraft, Thick Skin on Far Shoal; Cold Blood, Third Holster, Lucky Strike
on Wreck Cay. Their numbers are data and are read where they act (player,
session, power-ups). The perks carried show as coloured tags on the HUD.

An island boss leaves the perk power-up a quarter of the time: a random
perk for everyone, over the limit too.

Admin commands /switch and /perk, check tools/perk_check.tscn. Online the
host decides the power state (never run between two machines).

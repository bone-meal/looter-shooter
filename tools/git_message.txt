Polish1 D5a part 3: skins menu and per-gun camo challenges

C on a gun in the inventory opens the Gunsmith skins menu: a tier ladder (BO3 style, each tier opens after enough of the one below), a turning preview of the gun wearing the camo under the mouse, challenge tiles with progress, the locker of unboxed camos, apply and remove. Kills with a rolled gun count on its open challenges (57 draft challenges in data/camo_challenges.json) and earned camos are called out. Other players now see the camo on the gun in your hands.

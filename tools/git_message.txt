Shared colosseum runs online, perks that fit the game, modifier texts

The colosseum run is now one run for the whole party: the host runs every
wave and the others see the same enemies, hit them (the host pays the
points) and each rolls his own loot. Wave, enemies left and the clear are
shown on every machine; nobody left in the arena ends the run.

Found and fixed with a new two-copies-on-one-PC online test: once a single
island enemy was gone on the host, the host stopped sending enemy positions
(a freed enemy in a typed variable stopped the update every frame), so
teammates saw frozen stand-ins that never went away; the same in Aurelind.
A joiner also no longer runs a round of its own in its first moments.

Perks: Trade Winds also stops anything slowing you, Blast Proof halves
fire and blast damage, Run and Gun lets you aim at full speed, Cold Blood's
quiet first shot also has no spread.

Colosseum board: every modifier says exactly what it does, under the list
when you point at it and in its tooltip.

Round 6 step 8a: the archipelago (four islands in a ring, bridges, debris)

The single spawn island becomes four islands round a lagoon, all from
data/archipelago.json: Home Sand (radius 40 m), Painter's Key, Far Shoal and
Wreck Cay (26 m each), joined by two plank bridges and two sandbars. Each way
is blocked in its middle by debris that costs points to clear (750, 2250,
6750, 20250; the dearest closes the ring back to the spawn island) and closes
again at game over. The colosseum painting now stands on Painter's Key behind
the first debris; the Aurelind painting, bank, crow and lifeguard stay on the
spawn island.

Pure land rules in core/archipelago.gd (heights, which island, washing up,
where enemies may come out of the sea, debris state). Enemies come up round
the island their prey is on, on its side, and one left behind on another
island comes again near the players. Wading too deep carries a player back
to the shore, so nobody walks round a debris. Rocks and plants are drawn
only near their island: the spawn view costs 13.9 ms on Forward+ at 1080p
Ultimate (14.4 before), 4.9 ms on Compatibility.

Online the host clears debris for everybody after checking that the asker
stands at it (never run between two machines).

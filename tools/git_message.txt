Round 6 step 8b + 8c: wall buys, shelters with boarded windows, carpenter

Wall buys: eight tarred boards with a chalk outline of the gun and its
price (pistol 500 and shotgun 1000 on Home Sand, AK 1200 and M16 1200 on
Painter's Key, sniper 1500 and grenade launcher 2000 on Far Shoal, M60 2500
and rocket launcher 3000 on Wreck Cay). The plain gun takes a free ready
slot or the place of the gun in hand, which goes to the backpack; holding
that kind of gun already buys a full ammo pool for half the price. The
outlines are drawn from our own gun models (tools/chalk_shot.tscn and
tools/make_chalk_guns.py).

Shelters: three driftwood huts (Home Sand, Far Shoal, Wreck Cay) with a door
toward the island and a boarded window in each other wall. An enemy whose
prey is inside walks to the nearest window, tears a board every 1.2 s and
climbs in (at most 6 inside a hut); when the prey leaves they step out of
the door one by one. Holding interact at a window nails boards back for 10
points each (12 paid per round). New power-up Shipwright (carpenter): every
window whole again and 200 points each. Pure rules in core/shelter_rules.gd.

Also: an island enemy that falls out of the world comes out of the sea
again. Online the host decides boards and checks who nails them (never run
between two machines).

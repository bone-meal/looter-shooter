Polish1 D5c part 9: Void Howler, the eighth boss mask

An armoured wolf in dark steel plates: a long flat-topped muzzle and an open snarling jaw full of metal teeth, blade ears swept back, a segmented crest down the skull, purple light in the seams and black void eyes ringed with purple. In the game runes orbit its head (one glyph ring turned by its shader), void fog spills from its jaws and the eye rings swirl. Steel in the mask sculpts no longer browns in its corners when it is meant to have no rust.

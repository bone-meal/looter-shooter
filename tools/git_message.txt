Sea step 8a: random encounters, pirate ships with broadsides, floating cargo

Encounter rolls by region, weather and night outside the home port's safe waters; pirate sloops and brigs sail to lay the target abeam and fire their broadsides; a sunk pirate leaves barrels, crates and a sea chest afloat with doubloons, materials and a chance of a gun.
Pirate look: dark sails, black pennant, our own flag. Cannon balls hurt players they land near.
Online: foes ride in the fleet states, the client fleet rebuilds only from the first ship that differs; floating cargo is decided by the host. Two-copy tested.

Round 6 step 9b + 9c: mystery box, upgrade machine, traps

Mystery box: 950 points a spin, three seconds later it offers a rolled gun
(green or better, luck grows with the round, never pony) for ten seconds.
After four to eight spins a seagull carries the box to another island. It
works without power. The gun takes a free ready slot, else the place of the
gun in hand, else it lies at your feet.

Upgrade machine on Far Shoal (needs power): interact rolls the gun in hand
anew at the same tier for 5000 points; the reload key makes it one tier
higher and rolls it anew for 10000 times the new tier's number, up to
rainbow. A plain gun becomes a Common one for 5000. Hand-made guns are
refused.

Traps (need power, no points for their kills): a Storm Coil on each hut
(1000 points, 25 s, 60 s cooldown) takes half the health of enemies round
the hut every quarter second, and a Fire Pit on Painter's Key (750 points,
20 s) burns enemies and players standing in it.

Pure rules in core/mystery_box.gd and core/trap_state.gd. Online the host
decides box and traps (never run between two machines).

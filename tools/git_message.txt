Sea step 8e-3: the Whorl Kraken

Region 7's kraken surfaces beside the ship and sends four arms up her sides:
while any arm holds her she cannot move; each arm rears and smashes the deck
(players knocked flat, hull damage); arms are shot off one by one and grow back;
killing the body sinks its arms and floats its hoard. Own models (chained arm
segments), loot, items. Two-copy tested (set kraken).

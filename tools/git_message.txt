Chase guns: red text, signature behaviours, gimmick models; the Mixtape

First chase gun from the approved list (diary/DESIGN_UNIQUES.md): the
Mixtape, an M16 with a modelled MP3 player duct-taped to the receiver
(screen, click wheel, tape, earbuds). While it is in hand it plays your
own Music folder shuffled, as 3D sound from you; its screen scrolls the
song name; the inspect key skips to the next song and turns the gun to
show the screen. Red text on the card (the director's words). Framework
for the rest: red / signature / attach / pool fields in
data/gun_uniques.json, game/signatures/, game/unique_attach.gd,
tools/blender/unique_attach.py. Others hearing it online comes next.

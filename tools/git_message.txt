Round 6 step 5: the island fights back in endless rounds

Ten seconds after you stand on the spawn island round 1 begins: enemies
wade out of the sea, more and tougher every round (health, speed and
damage, data/island_rounds.json), a boss every fifth round, a round of
fast sea crawlers from round 7 with max ammo at its end, runners from
round 10, swimmers that surface at your own beach, and crawlers left by
blasts. The HUD shows the round in chalk and the enemies left; going down
alone restarts at round 1; left alone for five seconds you heal. Island
enemies drop no guns. Enemies now walk around what blocks them.
Online the host runs the island's enemies and pays every hit and kill
(game/net_rounds.gd; written, not yet run between two machines).
tools/round_check.tscn checks the rules; /round n jumps to a round.

Card game online: player tables, legendary figures

- Three player card tables online: sit, the other player sits, both pick a deck, 5 s countdown, the host runs the match (core/tcg/table_book.gd, game/net_tcg.gd, game/tcg/player_table.gd)
- Clients hold a mirror of the host's match and send only intents; everyone sees seats and matches (spectators see all)
- Walking off or dropping out mid-match loses it; a win against a player records the result, pays 50 chips and gives the win-milestone mats
- Small 3D figures of legendary creatures stand on their cards (per graphics tier, none on Potato); your leader card shows above your hand
- Tested on one PC with two copies of the game: a full match, the winner got the record and 50 chips
- Dev: !go club, !tcg ptable / pdeck / pmove / pnote / put; tools/pair_set.txt picks the pair test command set

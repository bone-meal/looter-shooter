Round 6 step 10: weather by round, buildable shield and boat, Gull Rock

Weather and light follow the round (data/island_weather.json, pure
core/weather_rules.gd, game/island_weather.gd): fog from round 10, a storm
with rain, lightning and thunder from round 20, a red sky while a boss round
is fought. Worked out from the round number alone, so nothing is sent
online. Rain is one MultiMesh whose shader moves the drops.

Buildables (data/buildables.json, core/build_state.gd, game/build_site.gd):
parts lie on the islands under warm beams, found for the whole party; each
thing is built at its bench. Game over puts every part back.

Shield (data/shield.json, core/shield_state.gd, game/shield_model.gd): on the
back it stops hits from behind, held up (key X) it stops hits from ahead and
the shoot key bashes; 15 hits, a boss's blow costs 3; a new one is taken at
the bench. Other players see it in the hand or on the back.

Boat (data/boat.json, core/boat_rules.gd, game/boat.gd, game/boat_model.gd):
built at the slipway on Wreck Cay, steered by whoever sits at the tiller with
the move keys; six seats, nobody shoots from it; it runs aground in shallow
water and backs off; you get off onto the nearest sand. Online the host sails
it from the helmsman's keys (never run between two machines).

Gull Rock: a fifth island no bridge reaches. A backpack lost there washes up
at the slipway.

Also: /build command, dev steps !weather !build !shield !boat,
tools/build_check.tscn, six new sounds.

Shared slot jackpot for the whole lobby

Online the slot pot is the lobby's, kept by the host (game/net_slots.gd): a client's spin is an intent, the host rolls it, grows the one pot and pays a jackpot out of it at once; the pot is sent to everyone after every spin and a jackpot is told to the lobby chat. A client keeps its own saved pot aside; a refused or unanswered spin gives the chips back. Pair set slotpot: the guest saw the host's spin grow the pot, the guest's jackpot reset it on both machines.

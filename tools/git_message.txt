Sea step 8d: ghost ships and the drowned at night

Ghost ships phase in and out: faded = cannon balls fly through her, solid only
to fire her broadside; her ghost crew boards like pirates; she fades away with
her men at dawn; sunk or struck = spectral loot.
The drowned rise round a ship at night in waves, climb her hull anywhere and
fight on deck; in the water they hold swimmers and drag them under.
Fix: only land runs a ship aground (a boarder no longer stops dead under our sails).

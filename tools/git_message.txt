Polish1 D4d part 1: tree enemies climb, glide and cut ropes

Climbers go up trunks and stilts to the treetop decks by planned routes
(climb spots per deck, bridges, stairs and catwalks between), gliders
come out of the sky and swoop, rope-cutters go for bought rope bridges
and ladders and cut them (players mend them free by holding interact).
Tough climbing versions of the drowned, runner and brute. While the prey
stands up high a share of the round (and every crab) comes from that
layer's tree mix. No melee through the planks. Models are stand-ins
until part 2.

Round 5 batch 6: trading between players and shared player drops

Online a gun a player drops is laid down by the host for everyone and goes
to the first who picks it up (enemy and chest drops stay each player's
own). Trading: hold interact on a player or /trade <name>, the other
accepts with interact; two columns of guns and armour, ready ticks cleared
by any change, both confirm; Esc, walking apart or leaving cancels. Rules
in core/trade_desk.gd and core/shared_drops.gd (tools/trade_check.tscn);
the host only judges that a thing is a legal roll.

ivo: Ivo Brack | The fog took the boat and left me the oar. Generous of it. | Lanternwick! Lights in every window. I'll never complain about lamp oil again. | I row the night ferry now. Only as far as the lanterns reach, mind. | Don't follow the little lights over the water. Trust me on that.
senna: Senna Moorhen | I could hear your bell before I could see you. Ring it again, it's lovely. | They say the Lantern Hall never goes dark. mott: Mott Peat | I was cutting peat on the bog and the bog cut back. | Solid planks under my feet. I could weep. I won't. I'll have a drink. | I cut pitch now, on the Knots. It's like peat but it fights harder. | The orchard used to give apples. Now it gives eels. Still fruit, if you squint.
lark: Lark Reedley | You found me by the reeds! I'd started teaching the herons to talk. | A kitchen! Give me a pot and a fish and stand well back. | I cook eel nine ways at the hall. The tenth way is a secret. | Bring me a heron's egg and I'll show you the tenth way. Kidding. Mostly.

[riddles]
unwatched_wick: A lamp on a mound burns with no hand to trim it, / north and east of the town where every window glows. / Sail with the morning a long throw past it, a little south, and look down where the lamplight cannot reach.
lone_fist: A single mangrove makes a fist of its roots in open water, / north and west of the town where every window glows. / On its northern side, a short swim out, the roots let go of what they held.
dry_barge: A barge that ran aground and never ran again, / between the bog and the knotted roots. / Behind her stern, toward the setting sun, she dropped her last cargo for you.
handless_bell: A bell rings out there with no hand on its rope, / south and east of the knotted roots, on a buoy no one ties to. / Wait till it rings, then swim south of it. The sea floor answers.
far_reeds: Reeds stand in the water far to the north, / north of where the orchard drowned its feet. / Leave them toward the morning, a little north, and you will not leave empty-handed.
western_fist: The mangrove at the western edge of the fog grips the sea alone. / Go south and east of it, as the tide goes out. / What the roots would not keep, the sand did.
fog_bank: A bank of sand that the fog wears like a coat, / east of the hollow giant, at the fog's southern edge. / Just north of where it ends, the coat has a pocket.
last_wick: The last lantern burns at the far north-west of the Fog Banks. / North and east of it, a short swim, before its light gives out. / Someone hid a light of their own down there.
heron_reeds: Reeds stand south of Lanternwick where the herons fish. / Swim west of them as far as a heron would fly without tiring. / The herons know. They will not tell.
hermit: Far in the south-east a hermit keeps his camp under one mangrove. / He never swims south. You should, a little way. / He thinks no one knows. Now you do.

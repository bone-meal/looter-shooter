# THE ISLANDS' MAIN QUEST  (a secret quest: nothing shows until the first step is found)
# ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# FILE FOR WRITERS. The game reads this file as it is: save it and start the game to see your words.
#   * Lines starting with # are notes for you. The game ignores them.
#   * Change only the words AFTER the first colon of a line.   name: The Drowned Bell  ->  name: Bell of the Deep
#   * Do not change what is in [square brackets] or before the colon: that is how the game finds the line.
#   * One line per text. Long lines are fine. No quotation marks are needed. Inside jokes welcome.
#   * A "says:" line may be repeated: each one is shown in the chat, in order, one after another.
#
# EVERY STEP HAS:
#   what:   WHAT IS GOING ON. Shown at the top left of the screen while the step is the one to do.
#   where:  WHERE it happens, in a few words (shown after the what line).
#   who:    WHO speaks the says lines (shown before each line in the chat). May be empty.
#   says:   WHAT IS SAID when the step BEGINS (one line each, in order).
#   done:   the line shown when the step is finished.
#
# THING / HOW notes (polish1 D4f): under each step a "# THING:" note says what stands in the world for it (its model,
# tools/blender/quest_main.py) and how it moves, and "# HOW:" says what the players do. They are notes: the game reads
# only the lines without #. Rewrite them too if you want a thing changed or gone, and send the file back: the models
# follow your words.
#
# WHAT FINISHES A STEP and WHERE ITS THINGS STAND is not here: it is in data/zombies/quest.json (the designers'
# file). The steps below are in the same order. If the game cannot read a step here, it names it when the game
# starts ("quest.txt: step jars has no what line") and tools/colosseum_check.tscn lists it too.
# ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[quest main]
# The quest's name: shown once when it starts and over the tracker line.
name: The Drowned Bell
# Shown when the whole quest is done, before the reward.
finished: The bell is silent. For one night, the sea keeps its dead.
# Shown with the reward.
reward: The Keeper's chest washes up at your feet.

[step log]
# THING: a sea-stained leather logbook lying open against a driftwood stump in a little mound of sand, a brass clasp,
#   a faint blue glint on its last page (the map). Its pages flip when it is read.
# HOW: walk up to it on Home Sand (south-west) and press interact. This is how the quest is found: nothing shows before.
what: Read the keeper's logbook
where: in the sand on Home Sand, south-west
who: Keeper's logbook
says: Day 41. The bell rang again last night. There is no bell on these islands.
says: Day 44. They come out of the water when it rings. They walk like they remember the way.
says: Day 45. If anyone reads this: light the islands, and the old lamps, and take a boat to the rock.
done: The logbook's last page is a map of a rock far out at sea.

[step power]
# THING: the power switch and its parts (they already exist).
# HOW: find the switch's parts and throw the switch. If the power is already on, this step passes at once.
what: Bring light back to the islands
where: the power switch (its parts lie on Home Sand)
who: Keeper's logbook
says: The switch is on Painter's Key. The parts are where the storm left them.
done: The islands hum. Somewhere far out, the bell answers.

[step jars]
# THING: three clay jars bound with rope on a plank rack by the wreck; a crack down each jar's front where glowing
#   blue-green brine shows. The brine rises in the cracks and glows brighter with every kill.
# HOW: kill enemies close to the jars (15 kills within 8 m).
what: Fill the brine jars on Wreck Cay
where: the jars by the wreck on Wreck Cay
who: Keeper's logbook
says: The drowned carry the sea in them. Let them spill it into the jars.
done: The jars are full and glowing.

[step lamps]
# THING: three crooked wooden posts, each with a crossarm and a hanging iron lantern that burns warm yellow. A shot
#   lantern jerks, swings itself still and goes dark.
# HOW: shoot the three lanterns (Painter's Key, Far Shoal, Home Sand). Only this step's lanterns take shots.
what: Shoot out the three old lamps
where: on posts on Painter's Key, Far Shoal and Home Sand
who: Keeper's logbook
says: The old lamps guide them home. Put them out.
done: The last lamp bursts. The bell rings once, very close.

[step spyglass]
# THING: the keeper's brass spyglass on a wooden tripod at the very top of Wreck Cay's giant tree (the top ring deck,
#   19 m up). Looked through, it turns slowly to point at Gull Rock.
# HOW: climb Wreck Cay's treetop village to its top ring (buy the ladders) and press interact at the spyglass.
what: Look through the keeper's spyglass
where: the top of Wreck Cay's great tree
who: Keeper's logbook
says: From the top of the tree you can see the rock. I watched them walk out of the sea there every night.
done: Far out past Wreck Cay: a black rock, and a bell hanging in chains. The chains have a lock.

[step reforge]
# THING: the Re-forge on the oil rig's main deck (it already exists, with its own quest: the lighthouse, the cliff
#   foghorn and the tide wheel). If it is already awake, this step passes at once.
# HOW: wake the Re-forge: power the lighthouse (find its lens), crank the foghorn and turn the tide wheel.
what: Wake the Re-forge
where: the oil rig's main deck (light the lighthouse, the foghorn and the tide wheel)
who: Keeper's logbook
says: The lock was cast in the old forge out on the rig. Only the forge can cut its key. Wake it.
done: The Re-forge roars. Its hammer is waiting.

[step key]
# THING: the bell key: a long iron key with a glowing brine-blue bit. The Re-forge's hammer strikes it out on the anvil
#   and it rises over the forge's tray, turning and glowing, until the bell is opened.
# HOW: stand at the awake Re-forge (power on) and press interact.
what: Forge the bell key
where: at the Re-forge on the oil rig
who: Keeper's logbook
says: Lay the jars' brine on the anvil. Strike it. A key of the sea, for a lock of the sea.
done: The key glows like the jars. It is cold to hold.

[step boat]
# THING: the slipway and the boat's parts (they already exist; the skiff builds piece by piece).
# HOW: collect the boat's parts and build it at the slipway.
what: Build the boat
where: the slipway on Wreck Cay (its parts lie on three islands)
who: Keeper's logbook
says: The rock is past Wreck Cay, where no bridge goes. Build a boat.
done: The boat floats.

[step bell]
# THING: the drowned bell on Gull Rock: a big barnacled bronze bell with a green band, hanging from a driftwood beam
#   between two stone piers, bound by chains to the piers with a padlock whose keyhole glows brine-blue. The key drops
#   the chains; the bell swings and tolls three times across the sea.
# HOW: sail to Gull Rock and press interact at the bell.
what: Unlock the bell on Gull Rock
where: Gull Rock, past Wreck Cay (by boat)
who: Keeper's logbook
says: There it is. Do not ring it. Ring it.
done: The chains fall. The bell rings.

[step warden]
# THING: the Bell Warden (the tyrant boss model) rises out of the sea on Gull Rock, as strong as a boss ten rounds on.
# HOW: beat it.
what: Defeat what answers the bell
where: Gull Rock
who: The Bell Warden
says: Who rings my bell?
says: Then you will hear it forever.
done: The Bell Warden sinks back into the sea.

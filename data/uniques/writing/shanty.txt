Oh, the tide went out and it took my boots,
And the gulls came down for the rest,
So I sailed away on a barrel of salt
With a crab for a hat on my chest.
Now the captain's lost and the compass spins,
And the cook only boils the sea,
But we sing all night and we row all day
For the bottle that's floating to me.
If you find this note on a windy beach,
Pass it on to the next one along,
For the sea keeps half of whatever it gets
And the other half's left in this song.
